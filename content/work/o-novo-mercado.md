## The Problem

O Novo Mercado is Brazil's largest digital marketing ecosystem. The engineering team was moving fast but without the infrastructure to move *confidently*. No shared component system, no experimentation tooling, no reliable way to know if what they shipped actually worked.

## My Role

Senior Product Engineer with end-to-end ownership across multiple product squads. I worked closely with the CEO and Product leadership on prioritisation and delivery, and led the XP adoption that changed how the team worked.

## What We Built

**Vue Component Library**

A fully-tested, documented component library (Vitest + Histoire) used across all product squads. It eliminated the "build it again" problem that had been slowing every team down.

- Reduced UI development time by 40% across squads
- Served a platform with 300k+ monthly active users
- Full test coverage with Histoire for visual documentation

**Experimentation Infrastructure**

Introduced feature flags and Amplitude-driven experimentation to the platform. Something that hadn't existed before.

- Defined the experimentation workflow from zero
- Ran activation, engagement, and monetization funnel experiments
- Directly influenced an **18% uplift in subscription conversion**

**Platform Ownership**

Owned the end-to-end frontend of the VOD platform and freelancer marketplace: subscriptions, content delivery, community flows, and marketplace transactions.

## Engineering Approach

Led XP adoption across the engineering team. Not as a mandate, but by demonstrating it worked. Pair programming, test-first development, continuous integration. The team's delivery speed and code quality both improved measurably.

## Outcome

18% subscription conversion uplift. 40% reduction in UI development time. A codebase that multiple squads could contribute to confidently. Three years that built the foundation for everything after.
